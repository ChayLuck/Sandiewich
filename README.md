# Sandiewich

A first-person "red light, green light" survival game with a side of dark comedy, built in Godot 4.

Sit across the table from a monster and build a sandwich right under its nose — but only while it isn't watching.

The moment it turns to look at you, freeze. Click anything while it's watching and it shoots. Build fast and clean: your score is ingredients used × speed × combo, so the faster you assemble a sandwich without breaking the order, the more it's worth.

Stay frozen through a watch and you'll get paid for every sandwich you've eaten since your last reward. Spend that money on extra ingredients for bigger (riskier) sandwiches, a combo-boosting potion, or a 50/50 gambling coin. Mess up the order and you'll have to hit the reset button to try again — no judgment, it happens to everyone.

One mistake ends the run. Everything resets. Only your best score survives.

## Features

- Build sandwiches in the right order before the monster notices
- Score = ingredients × speed × combo — go fast, stay smooth
- Unlock ice cream and chocolate for bigger (and riskier) sandwiches
- Gamble, boost your combo, or just chase your own high score
- Normal or Beginner difficulty, so it's easy to learn the ropes
- Mouse or touch — plays in-browser on desktop and mobile

## Controls

Click / tap only.

## What's new in 1.1.0

- **New: Difficulty setting.** Choose Normal (the original challenge) or Beginner — a more forgiving monster (longer safe time, shorter danger time), a longer combo grace period, and a slower speed countdown. Switch anytime from Settings, on the main menu or mid-run.
- **New: Hover feedback.** Ingredients, the plate, shop items, and the reset button now give a little visual nudge when your cursor (or finger) is over them.
- **Tutorial updated** with a new Tips page pointing out the difficulty setting.
- **Fixed:** a visual glitch where the Back button in Settings poked outside its panel.
